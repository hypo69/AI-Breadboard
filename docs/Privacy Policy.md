# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 9, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **AI Assistant**, developed by **hypo69**, handles user information when you use the application available through [**https://davidka.net**](https://davidka.net).

AI Assistant currently uses Google Sign-In solely to authenticate users and provide access to the application.

The application is designed to collect and retain only the information necessary to provide user authentication, account identification, account management, and authenticated access.

## 2. Information We Access from Google

When you sign in to AI Assistant using Google Sign-In, the application accesses basic Google Account information required to authenticate and identify your account.

Depending on the information provided by Google during the authentication process, this may include:

- your email address;
- your name;
- your Google profile picture URL.

AI Assistant uses this information only to authenticate the user, identify the user's account, display basic account information, and maintain the user's account.

AI Assistant does **not** access or read the contents of:

- Gmail;
- Google Drive;
- Google Docs;
- Google Sheets;
- Google Calendar;
- Google Contacts;
- Google Photos;
- or other Google services or Google Account content.

AI Assistant does not request or use your Google Account password.

The application currently uses Google solely as an identity provider for user authentication and basic account identification.

## 3. How We Use Google User Data

The basic Google Account information received during Google Sign-In is used only for:

- authenticating the user;
- identifying the user's account;
- creating and maintaining the user's AI Assistant account;
- displaying the user's name and profile picture where applicable;
- maintaining the user's authenticated session;
- supporting account management and security.

Google user data is **not used for advertising or targeted advertising**.

Google user data is **not sold, rented, or provided to data brokers or information resellers**.

Google user data is **not used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models**.

Google user data is **not transmitted to AI models or other artificial intelligence services**.

## 4. Information We Store

AI Assistant stores only the information necessary to maintain the user's account and provide authenticated access to the application.

The information currently stored may include:

- the user's email address;
- the user's name;
- the URL of the user's Google profile picture;
- the date and time when the user's account was created;
- the date and time of the user's most recent login.

The email address, name, and Google profile picture URL are obtained through the Google authentication process.

The account creation timestamp and most recent login timestamp are generated and maintained by AI Assistant.

This information is used for account identification, authentication, account management, security, and displaying basic account information within the application.

AI Assistant does **not** store copies of Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Calendar data, contacts, photos, or other Google Account content because the application does not access such information.

## 5. Data Sharing

AI Assistant does not sell, rent, or otherwise commercially disclose Google user data.

Google user data is **not transferred or disclosed to third parties**, except where such disclosure is required by applicable law.

Google user data is not provided to:

- advertising networks;
- data brokers;
- information resellers;
- AI or machine-learning services;
- third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

The application currently does not transfer Google user data to external AI models or AI services.

## 6. Data Retention

AI Assistant retains the user's account and authentication information until the user deletes their account or requests deletion of their data.

The stored information is used only to maintain the user's account, provide authenticated access, support account management, and maintain application security.

When the user deletes their account or requests deletion of their data, the associated account information will be deleted, except where retention is required by applicable law or is necessary for legitimate security or fraud-prevention purposes.

## 7. Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their AI Assistant account and the personal information associated with that account.

To request deletion, the user should contact **hypo69** through the contact information provided on [**https://davidka.net**](https://davidka.net) and request deletion of their AI Assistant account and associated data.

After receiving a valid deletion request, the user's stored account and authentication information will be deleted, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Users may also remove AI Assistant's connection to their Google Account through their Google Account settings. Removing this connection prevents the application from continuing to use the Google authentication connection.

## 8. Data Security

AI Assistant stores account and authentication information in a **protected database**.

Access to the database is restricted and protected by appropriate security controls.

Reasonable technical and organizational security measures are used to protect stored user information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

The application uses secure communication mechanisms to protect data transmitted between the user's browser and the application.

AI Assistant does not store Google Account passwords.

## 9. Cookies and Sessions

AI Assistant may use cookies or similar browser technologies that are necessary to maintain an authenticated user session.

These technologies are used to provide login functionality and maintain the user's authenticated state.

They are not used for targeted advertising or cross-site advertising tracking.

## 10. Third-Party Services

AI Assistant currently uses **Google Sign-In** as an authentication provider.

Google Sign-In is used only for authentication and basic account identification.

The application does not currently use Google APIs to access or process user content from Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, contacts, photos, or other Google services.

No Google user content is accessed, stored, or processed by AI Assistant.

AI Assistant does not transmit Google user data to external AI models or other AI services.

Google's handling of information associated with Google Sign-In is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

## 11. Data Use Restrictions

AI Assistant does not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to providing the application's authentication and account-management functionality.

In particular, Google user data is not used for:

- targeted advertising;
- personalized advertising;
- retargeted advertising;
- selling data to data brokers;
- providing data to information resellers;
- marketing purposes;
- training generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models;
- making credit-worthiness or lending decisions.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of AI Assistant change.

If AI Assistant begins accessing additional Google Account data, using Google user data for additional purposes, storing additional information, or sharing information with third parties, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will always be published on this page.
